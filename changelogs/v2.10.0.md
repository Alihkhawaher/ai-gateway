# v2.10.0 — 2026-11-11

## Added
- **The app's thinking choice now reaches LM Studio.** Apps switch a model's thinking on or off in different ways, and LM Studio's `/v1/chat/completions` only honours `reasoning_effort`. On LM Studio endpoints the gateway now copies the app's own choice into that field:
  - `chat_template_kwargs.enable_thinking` and `thinking: {"type": "disabled"}` become `reasoning_effort: "none"`.
  - `reasoning.enabled` or `reasoning.effort` and Ollama's `think` become `"none"` or the level asked for.
  - Nothing is added when the app asks for nothing, and an app's own `reasoning_effort` is never changed. Other endpoints get the request exactly as sent.
  - This was tested with a Gemma 4 model: with `reasoning_effort: "none"`, LM Studio skipped thinking and still made the right tool call. It ignored the other fields.
- **The log and `GET /stats` show what the app asked for**, for example `thinking off (asked)`, or `think` in `/stats`. That shows whether an app sends a thinking choice at all.
- **Tool call healing can be switched on in Settings.** The endpoint dialog has **Heal tool calls** and **Check commands before they run (guard)**, and the endpoint table has a **Heal** column. Before, this was only possible in `config.json`. Saving keeps the endpoint's other `tool_heal` settings, such as `allow_rules`, and the change applies to the next request.
